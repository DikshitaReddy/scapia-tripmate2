About this prototype

Tripmate is a product concept built for a Scapia product internship assignment. Calendar, flights, booking partners, community membership and rewards are simulated. No personal calendar data or payment details are transmitted.

Tripmate is an independent prototype concept for evaluation, not an official Scapia product. Demo data only. No live calendar, flight, payment or rewards integrations.
